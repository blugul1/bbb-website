Why Trust BBB? Five Steps to Clearer Property Information

1

Listing Submission

Property information is submitted for inclusion on the BBB platform.

2

Information Review

Available property details are reviewed and recorded for the listing.

3

Document Checking

Where document checking has been completed, the listing displays the corresponding verification status.

4

Site Visit

Where a physical site visit has been completed, that status is also displayed.

5

Visible Verification Level

Each listing identifies its current status: Documents Checked, Site Visited, or Both Complete.

Important: Verification status describes the checks recorded by BBB. It does not establish a legal title guarantee, confirm the absence of disputes or replace verification with the relevant revenue authorities. Owners' personal documents and CNIC information are never published.
