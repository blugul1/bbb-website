About Us

What BBB is, how it works, and who leads it.

BBB — Bhimber Business Bureau is a property sale and purchase platform serving District Bhimber, Azad Kashmir. We help people explore property opportunities, connect with sellers and access clearer information before making a decision.

Property decisions involve more than finding a location or agreeing on a price. Buyers want to know what they are considering. Sellers want to reach genuine interested parties. Overseas Pakistanis need a clearer picture of the local property market before making decisions from a distance.

BBB — Bhimber Business Bureau was established to bring these needs together through a locally focused property platform. We cover the three tehsils of District Bhimber — Bhimber, Barnala and Samahni — including their villages and surrounding communities.

Our listings cover residential plots, houses, shops, commercial properties and agricultural land. Each listing carries a clearly displayed verification level, indicating whether its documents have been checked, its site has been visited, or both steps have been completed.

We believe property information should be presented honestly. A verification label tells you what has been checked; it should never be mistaken for a legal guarantee or a substitute for independent due diligence.

Why Trust BBB? Five Steps to Clearer Property Information

1

Listing Submission

Property information is submitted for inclusion on the BBB platform.

2

Information Review

Available property details are reviewed and recorded for the listing.

3

Document Checking

Where document checking has been completed, the listing displays the corresponding verification status.

4

Site Visit

Where a physical site visit has been completed, that status is also displayed.

5

Visible Verification Level

Each listing identifies its current status: Documents Checked, Site Visited, or Both Complete.

Important: Verification status describes the checks recorded by BBB. It does not establish a legal title guarantee, confirm the absence of disputes or replace verification with the relevant revenue authorities. Owners' personal documents and CNIC information are never published.

Property Matters Back Home — Better Information Before You Decide

Buying or selling property from overseas can be difficult when you cannot personally visit a location or review the available documents.

BBB is exploring remote-support services for overseas Pakistanis, including video-based property visit reports and document checking. At present, these services are at the interest-registration stage and have not yet launched.

If these services would be useful to you, register your interest through BBB's contact channels. Your interest will help us understand what overseas clients need before introducing the services.
